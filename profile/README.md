# Wise Repair

**Appliance service, supported by software built for the work.**

Wise Repair LLC provides residential and commercial appliance repair, diagnostics, installation, and replacement parts in Conway, Myrtle Beach, and surrounding South Carolina communities.

We are also developing **WiseRepair**, our connected service platform for the people who organize repairs, perform the work, and rely on their appliances every day.

[Visit our website](https://wise.repair/) · [Contact us](mailto:info@wise.repair)

## What we are building

Our software brings the service journey together, from the first request to the completed repair:

- **Service operations** — job management, scheduling, team coordination, and repair history.
- **Parts and inventory** — identifying, ordering, receiving, and tracking parts for each job.
- **Customer Workspace** — a connected place for customers to review estimates and invoices, follow appointments, and communicate with our team.
- **WiseRepair Technician** — a dedicated mobile application for field technicians, in development for iPhone and Android.
- **WiseRepair Customer** — a planned mobile application for easier access to service requests, updates, and repair records.

## Development status

WiseRepair is under active development. We are improving the web platform and building toward dedicated mobile experiences, guided by the needs of our service team and customers.

Mobile applications are not yet publicly available. We will share release announcements and official download links when they are ready.

This GitHub organization is home to our software development work. Our operational application is maintained in private repositories; public projects and documentation will appear here as they become available.
